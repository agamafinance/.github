# Agama Finance

**Private credit yield infrastructure on Stellar.**

Users deposit USDC into curated vaults and receive **agUSD**, a composable synthetic dollar (SEP-41) backed by diversified real-world credit pools from vetted originators. Staking agUSD produces **sagUSD**, a yield-bearing token that appreciates as private credit repayments generate returns on-chain.

### Live on Stellar Testnet

agUSD, sagUSD and six curated credit vaults are deployed and verifiable on [Stellar Expert](https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet), settled in native Circle USDC. Credit vaults are curated with [Qiro](https://www.qiro.fi/investor) and [Tenka](https://tenka.fi/).

### Repositories

**[agama-soroban](https://github.com/agamafinance/agama-soroban)** : core Soroban contracts in Rust. Vault, agUSD (SEP-41), sagUSD with DeFindex-compatible share accounting, Allocation Engine with on-chain concentration caps, and the Oracle Adapter consuming Reflector feeds. Apache-2.0.
